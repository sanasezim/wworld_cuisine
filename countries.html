Cuisines around the world

Every cuisine reflects its country's history, ingredients and traditions.

KZ

Kazakhstan

Traditional Kazakh cuisine reflects nomadic culture and hospitality.

  • Beshbarmak
  • Baursak
  • Kazy

Kazakh meals often include meat, dough and dairy products.

IT

Italy

Italian cuisine is known for fresh ingredients and simple recipes.

  • Pizza
  • Pasta
  • Risotto

Italian cooking uses tomatoes, olive oil, cheese and fresh herbs.

JP

Japan

Japanese cuisine values balance, presentation and seasonal ingredients.

  • Sushi
  • Ramen
  • Tempura

Japanese dishes often use rice, fish and seasonal vegetables.

MX

Mexico

Mexican cuisine combines tortillas, vegetables, beans and spices.

  • Tacos
  • Quesadillas
  • Enchiladas

Corn, beans and chilli peppers are common in Mexican cooking.

IN

India

Indian cuisine includes spices and many regional traditions.

  • Biryani
  • Dal
  • Naan

Indian regions use different spices, grains and vegetables.

KR

South Korea

Korean cuisine combines rice, vegetables and fermented foods.

  • Kimchi
  • Bibimbap
  • Tteokbokki

Korean meals often include rice, side dishes and fermented foods.
