HOME COOKING

Simple recipes.

Easy versions of popular dishes that you can prepare at home.

ITALY

Margherita Pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare and shape the dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce.
  3. Add mozzarella.
  4. Bake until ready.
  5. Add basil before serving.

MEXICO

Vegetable Tacos

Ingredients

  • Small tortillas
  • Beans
  • Corn
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Prepare vegetables.
  2. Warm beans and corn.
  3. Warm tortillas.
  4. Add all ingredients.
  5. Serve immediately.

SOUTH KOREA

Simple Bibimbap

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Carrot
  • Cucumber
  • Spinach
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Prepare the vegetables.
  3. Place rice in a bowl.
  4. Add vegetables.
  5. Add an egg and serve.
