Simple recipes

Three simple recipes inspired by different world cuisines.

Italy

Margherita Pizza

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough.
  2. Add tomato sauce.
  3. Add mozzarella.
  4. Bake until ready.
  5. Add basil.
Mexico

Vegetable Tacos

Ingredients

  • Tortillas
  • Beans
  • Corn
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce

Steps

  1. Prepare vegetables.
  2. Warm beans and corn.
  3. Warm tortillas.
  4. Add ingredients.
  5. Serve fresh.
South Korea

Simple Bibimbap

Ingredients

  • Cooked rice
  • Carrot
  • Cucumber
  • Spinach
  • Egg

Steps

  1. Cook rice.
  2. Prepare vegetables.
  3. Put rice in a bowl.
  4. Add vegetables.
  5. Add an egg.
